About Aroma Coffee and Tea house

Founded in 2021 in Kiambu, Aroma Coffee and Tea House was born from a love for great coffee and meaningful connections. We set out to create a cozy place where quality meets comfort. Today, we proudly serve Kiambu with freshly brewed coffee, soothing teas, and warm hospitality.

Our Mission

>Our mission at Aroma Coffee and Tea House is to serve exceptional coffee and tea made from the finest ingredients, while creating a warm and welcoming space where every guest feels at home. We are committed to quality, comfort, and genuine hospitality in every cup we serve.

Our vision

>Our vision is to be the most loved coffee and tea house in the community a place where people come together to share stories, find inspiration, and enjoy unforgettable flavors. We aspire to grow while staying true to our values of quality, warmth, and authentic hospitality.
